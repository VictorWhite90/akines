### Motion layer, added 29 Oct 2026
The user asked for interactive animations, scroll reveal animations for images and text, and other animations that make the site feel premium, then added staggered animations (several elements animating in sequence with a slight, overlapping delay instead of all at once). Built as a layer on top of the finished page; no component logic depends on it.
- Files: src/styles/motion.css (all keyframes and start states, imported last in layout.tsx), src/lib/motion.ts (plain DOM code: finds elements, tags them with data attributes, IntersectionObserver, hero scroll scrub, eased anchor scrolling; exports `initMotion` and `scrollToId`), src/components/Motion.tsx (client component that calls `initMotion` once, mounted at the end of the page). Also: `--ease-out` and `--ease-io` in tokens.css, rebuilt `.btn` and `.dlink` in base.css, underline draw-in on sign-in and footer links, a `words` prop on the `Lines` component (hero headline), exit animation in Menu.tsx (`data-closing`, 280 ms, then the section scroll starts), and an inline head script in layout.tsx that adds `js` to the html element before first paint and `hero-ready` when the hero photo has loaded (or after 4 s).
- Hero: photo fades and settles (scale 1.14 to 1 over 2.4 s) once loaded; header items, eyebrow, headline words (50 ms apart) and the two buttons arrive in sequence; scrolling out drifts and zooms the photo and lifts and fades the copy (CSS variable `--hp`, 0 to 1).
- Scroll reveals: three kinds set by `data-reveal`: `up` (text rises and fades), `words` (headline words rise one after another, 55 ms apart, via `<span class="w" style="--wi:n">`), `frame` (photographs and full-bleed sections open downwards behind an ivory curtain pseudo-element while the image settles from scale 1.16). Below-the-fold photos fade in when loaded (`data-loading`). A sweep reveals anything scrolled past unseen (fast flick, link jump).
- Stagger: elements that intersect in the same observer batch get `--rd` = index x 100 ms (max 8 steps), in reading order, counted per section or footer; animations last about 1.1 s so neighbours overlap. Frames reveal immediately (0 ms).
- Interaction: buttons fill with a rising sweep on hover and scale to 0.97 on press, `dlink` and footer or sign-in underlines redraw, panel/story/full-card photos zoom slowly on hover, trust chips lift, stepper rows and photos ease between states, trust stage copy lifts in with a delay, the menu panel, rules, links and actions animate in one by one and reverse on close, section links glide (duration min(1500, 650 + distance x 0.11) ms, easeInOutCubic, respects scroll-margin, stops on wheel, touch, key or mouse down).
- Safety: hidden start states exist only under the `js` class and `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`. With reduced motion `initMotion` returns before tagging anything, links jump, and layout is pixel-identical to the pre-motion build. Print shows everything. Hover effects are inside `@media (hover: hover)`. Only data attributes and CSS variables are set on elements (never classes), so React re-renders cannot undo them.
- Design decisions worth knowing: the frame reveal uses a curtain instead of `clip-path` because Chrome's IntersectionObserver treats content under an ancestor clip-path as not intersecting (tested); individual CSS transform properties (`scale`, `translate`) are used so reveal, hover and scroll effects compose without fighting over `transform`; reveal animations use `animation-fill-mode: backwards` so elements return to their natural state afterwards.
- To tweak: selector lists `UP`, `WORDS`, `FRAME` and constants `STAGGER_MS` (100) and `STAGGER_MAX` (8) at the top of motion.ts; durations and easing in motion.css.
- Verification: 51 of 51 original functional checks still pass; 23 of 23 motion checks (hero sequence, every element revealed after a slow scroll and after a jump, nothing left transparent, eased scroll shape and landing under the header, wheel interrupts the glide, hero scrub, menu exit, reduced motion tags nothing, mobile no overflow, no console errors or warnings); 9 of 9 stagger checks (hero words 50 ms apart, header items, headline words, community moments 0/100/200, trust chips and footer columns 100 ms apart, delays shorter than the duration so neighbours overlap). With reduced motion the page heights match the pre-motion build exactly (15546 at 1512, 12817 at 1194, 15016 at 402) with blurred pixel differences of at most 4 of 255.
- Known trade-offs: the first load after a deploy can show a photo fade in late while the image optimizer warms; parallax and curtain reveals add a little GPU work on very old phones (reduced motion switches everything off).

### Header hides on scroll down and returns on scroll up, added 29 Oct 2026 (05:45 Lagos)
The user asked: when scrolling down the header should hide by animating up the screen, then appear back when scrolling up by animating down, and remain fixed to the top when scrolling up.
- Behaviour: scrolling down slides the header up out of view (translate to -100% minus 2 px and the safe area, 0.45 s, `--ease-io`); scrolling up slides it back down (0.6 s, `--ease-out`, soft landing) and it stays fixed at the top while scrolling up. It needs 8 px of travel in one direction to react (`TRAVEL` in Header.tsx), so trackpad wobble does nothing. The scroll position is clamped to the real scroll range so rubber-banding at the top or bottom is not read as a direction.
- Always visible: within the first header height from the top (so over the hero it stays and keeps its clear then solid states), while the menu is open, when keyboard focus enters the header (`onFocus`, so tabbing or shift-tabbing back to it reveals it), and while a link glides to a section.
- Decision I took without asking: menu and in-section links (anything that calls `scrollToId`) are treated as navigation, not scrolling. `scrollToId` in src/lib/motion.ts sets `data-gliding` on the html element during the glide (and for 150 ms after, or around an instant jump), the header stays visible, and the section lands under it at the anchor offset with no empty gap. The next scroll down hides it. If the user prefers the header to hide during link glides too, remove the `data-gliding` check in Header.tsx (and app.js in the preview), but sections will then land with a 64 to 80 px empty band above them.
- Reduced motion: still hides and shows, but at once (the global reduced-motion rule sets transition duration to about 0), no slide.
- Files: src/components/Header.tsx (state `hidden`, `data-hidden` attribute, refs for travel and last position, `bar` ref), src/styles/header.css (`translate` transition and the `[data-hidden='true']` rule), src/lib/motion.ts (`data-gliding`).
