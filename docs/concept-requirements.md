# Artelia Connect — requirements checklist

Condensed from the *Artelia Connect Concept Note* (September 2026) and the
strategy memo that preceded it. Every screen in this prototype cites the IDs
it covers; `/concept` in the app maps each ID to the screens that satisfy it.

**The test for every feature:** does it help people form a meaningful creative
relationship or produce a credible creative outcome? If it mainly increases
screen time, it should not sit at the centre.

## P — Positioning & principles
- **P1** Build Connect *inside* Artelia. Three pillars: **Discover** (marketplace), **Connect** (human community), **Create** (artist workspace / collaboration).
- **P2** Purpose over consumption: no infinite-scroll feed. Prioritise intentions, rooms, projects and opportunities.
- **P3** Discovery through relevance and complementarity, not popularity. No follower counts, no public performance.
- **P4** "I buy African art" → "I belong to African creativity": the living digital home of African creativity.

## C — Core architecture
- **C1 Connection Intent** — at onboarding *and* session start: find inspiration, meet a collaborator, discover artists, discuss art, find an opportunity, learn, reflect quietly, feel less alone. Ask *why are you here*, not *what art do you like*.
- **C2 Creative DNA** — living profile from declared interests, practice and *consented* behaviour. Dimensions: creative curiosity, storytelling preference, historical interest, material affinity, emotional style, traditional↔contemporary, abstract↔figurative, collaboration style, silent observer↔conversation starter, collector motivation (Explorer, Investor, Historian, Decorator, Patron, Mentor). Explainable, editable, privacy-controlled.
- **C3 Meaningful Matching** — people whose creative journeys complement yours; every introduction explained ("You both collect northern Nigerian textile art"). Uses fit, availability, geography, language, readiness, trust, collaboration preferences.
- **C4 Presence Rooms** — small facilitated conversations, capped at six. No audience, no livestream, no followers. Artists sit with collectors, collectors with students, students with curators.
- **C5 Creative Circles** — sustained communities: African fashion, photography, digital art, textiles, ceramics, collectors, curators, diaspora, young artists, traditional arts. Moderated peer support, portfolio feedback, mentorship.
- **C6 Stories behind every artwork** — voice recording from the artist, studio video, inspiration, challenges, failures, meaning, music, poem, sketches, the evolution. People buy stories, not objects.
- **C7 Artist Journals** — living journal: today's sketch, failed painting, new idea, doubts, wins. Collectors witness the journey.
- **C8 Collector Journals** — why they bought, what changed, how it feels, where it lives, how children interact with it. Becomes part of the artwork's living history.
- **C9 Art Legacy** — every artwork accumulates memories: who created it, who owned it, where it travelled, exhibitions, awards, stories, audio reflections. Future owners inherit its history.
- **C10 Studio Visits** — virtual, live, 360°, VR. Meet artists in their studios, talk directly, buy.
- **C11 AI Creative Companion** — not a generic chatbot: African art history, curatorial knowledge, materials, techniques, symbolism, exhibition planning, pricing guidance, collector education, creative wellbeing.

## S — Collaboration Studio & Opportunity Engine
- **S1** Seven stages: Discover → Imagine → Agree → Create → Present → Share value → Preserve.
- **S2 Structured Open Calls** — standard fields: commissioning organisation, theme, eligible media, geography, experience level, number of works, budget or fee, rights requested, selection process, required portfolio materials, timetable. Artists apply with selected works and tailored statements, not links in comments.
- **S3 Curator Desk** — screen eligibility, review portfolios at scale, tag, compare candidates, private notes, invite additional material, longlists and shortlists, communicate status, retain consented profiles on a future-opportunity radar.
- **S4 Collaboration Matching** — similarity *and* productive difference (textile × sound, sculptor × architect, photographer × choreographer), clearly explained; private idea canvas before a formal project room.
- **S5 Fair collaboration by default** — agree contributions, background and new IP, creator credit, decision rights, expenses, revenue sharing, permitted uses, documentation and exit *before* production. Plain-language, configurable templates; professional advice recommended for complex projects.
- **S6 Project room** — boards, files, tasks, milestones and approvals in one place.
- **S7 Share value** — split instructions, expense logic and payment status stay visible.
- **S8 Preserve** — contribution ledger, creator credits, provenance and ownership history.
- **S9** Visible application status and clear communication stages for artists.

## W — Creative wellbeing & safety
- **W1** Private mood check-ins, daily mood canvas, creative burnout reflection, artist anxiety & collector reflection, expressive art journaling, breathing / grounding, mindfulness, gratitude.
- **W2** Referral pathways to licensed therapists and art therapists.
- **W3** Consent-based and private by default; separate from commercial ranking; never used to target sales, reduce opportunity access or infer medical conditions; the AI does not diagnose.
- **W4** Crisis language triggers a safety flow with locally relevant emergency guidance.

## T — Trust governance & responsible AI
- **T1** Identity and professional verification for curators, galleries, institutions and high-value commissioners.
- **T2** Clear distinction between verified facts, self-declared information and AI recommendations.
- **T3** Explainable matching with controls to edit interests, decline recommendations and adjust visibility.
- **T4** No sale of private wellbeing data; never used for advertising or opportunity ranking.
- **T5** Portfolio protection: watermarking options, reporting tools, evidence preservation.
- **T6** Scam and impersonation monitoring for open calls, payment requests and unsolicited opportunities.
- **T7** Community standards: harassment, exploitation, plagiarism, cultural appropriation, misrepresentation.
- **T8** Human review and appeals for consequential moderation or access decisions.

## B — Business model
- **B1** Free participation for artists (supply growth); monetise high-value professional workflows and successful outcomes — not a subscription on every artist.
- **B2** Streams: marketplace commission · collaboration facilitation fee · professional open calls · professional workspaces (pipeline, review, CRM, analytics) · collector membership (private rooms, previews, studio access, advisory) · project protection (agreement templates, escrow, rights records) · events & learning (portfolio reviews, salons, residencies, sponsored circles) · enterprise partnerships (white-labelled calls, programmes, impact reporting).

## M — Pilot & success measures
- **M1** Measures: meaningful connection, opportunity access, collaboration, artist income, collector depth, trust & safety, sustainability.
- **M2** 12-week pilot: 150–250 artists, 15–25 curators or galleries, 30 collectors, 3–5 institutional/brand partners; Nigeria plus selected African and diaspora hubs; 3 open calls, 20+ curated introductions, 10 collaboration canvases, 3–5 formal agreements; participant interviews.

## U — Initial use cases (each should be walkable in the prototype)
- **U1** Curator publishes a call for emerging West African figurative painters, screens portfolios, shortlists, retains consented artists for the future.
- **U2** Nigerian textile artist × Senegalese sound artist matched around migration and memory, test an idea privately, create an installation with 50:50 rights.
- **U3** Institution commissions a statement artwork, receives verified applications, reviews concepts, monitors milestones in a project room.
- **U4** Collector discovers an artist through a journal, joins a Presence Room, visits the studio virtually, becomes a long-term patron.
- **U5** Multidisciplinary creator finds a photographer, model and filmmaker without searching disconnected profiles.
- **U6** Isolated emerging artist joins a moderated peer circle, receives portfolio feedback, is guided toward credible opportunities.
